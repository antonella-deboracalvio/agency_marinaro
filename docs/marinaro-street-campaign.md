# Marinaro — street campaign

Visual originale creato per questa versione con lo strumento integrato di generazione immagini; nessuna API o dipendenza aggiunta al sito. È un concept fotografico di campagna, non una fotografia dei fondatori o di una campagna realmente affissa.

Il riferimento fornito è stato usato per l’idea del cartellone tipografico e del passante mosso. La scena, il personaggio e l’impaginazione del manifesto sono stati generati per Marinaro.

File usati dal componente `src/components/team/StudioProjectCard.jsx`:

- `src/assets/team/marinaro-street-campaign.webp`: 1254 × 1254 px.
- `src/assets/team/marinaro-street-campaign-small.webp`: 720 × 720 px.

Il formato WebP e la versione piccola mantengono leggera la card. Il prompt finale è riportato sotto.

## Prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: an original photographic campaign image for the Italian creative technology agency Marinaro Digital Studio, to fill a large website card. Generate a new image, not a screenshot or webpage. Use the attached reference only for the general idea of a typographic street poster and an anonymous pedestrian walking past; create distinct architecture, a new photographic composition, new poster layout and original person.
Photorealistic urban editorial photography, roughly square to 4:5 portrait composition. A large off-white advertising poster in a slim brushed aluminum rounded rectangular frame, mounted on a light grey concrete and narrow pale ceramic-tile city wall. Frame and poster occupy most of the left and central image and stay fully within the crop. A young adult man in a charcoal jacket with a practical black backpack walks past in the right foreground, captured with authentic horizontal motion blur from a slow shutter. He covers only a small part of the poster's right lower edge, and does not obscure any words. Natural overcast daylight, restrained black and white photography, real paper grain, brushed metal, slightly worn paving at the bottom. Strong editorial art direction, candid believable photo, no artificial 3D render.
Poster design: warm off-white paper with very bold black condensed grotesk typography. Large, meticulously legible headline occupying the upper half, exact text broken into these four lines:
"ARE PEOPLE"
"IGNORING"
"YOUR"
"BRAND?"
Lower third: a quiet very pale dusty lavender field with another large black headline on two lines, exact text:
"WE CAN"
"FIX THAT."
At the bottom of the poster, clear modest uppercase signature: "MARINARO" and beneath it smaller "DIGITAL STUDIO". Use a distinctive new typesetting layout and fresh spacing for this agency. All spelling exact. The entire main headline and the bottom statement must be readable at card size. A lot of confidence through typography, no extra slogans, no extra icons, no numbers, no arrows, no interface controls or expand button, no watermarks. Keep the overall photographic scene monochrome except for the subtle pale lavender poster accent. A high-end independent studio campaign, modern and alternative, strong and minimal, not glossy AI imagery.
